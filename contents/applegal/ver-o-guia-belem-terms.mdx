### What Ver-o-Guia Belém is

A tourist guide to Belém do Pará. It describes sights, markets, museums, parks and islands, the regional food, trips, ready-made plans for one to three days, and the festivals that happen every year, such as the Círio de Nazaré. It has a map, favourites and a list of the stops you have visited. It is an independent guide: it is not run by the city, the state, the places it describes or any tour operator, and it sells nothing and takes no bookings.

### Price

Ver-o-Guia Belém is free. It shows ads in two places, the middle of the home screen and the end of each place's page. There are no in-app purchases.

### The information in the guide

Descriptions, opening hours, prices and dates come from public sources: official pages, newspapers, Wikipedia and travel guides. Each practical detail shows its source and the date it was checked. Places change their hours and prices, close for works or holidays, and festivals move. Confirm before you go, especially for boat trips and anything that depends on the weather or the tide. When the guide has no current source, it says so. If you find something wrong, "Report wrong information" in Profile opens an email to me.

Distances and directions come from your phone and from Google Maps. The guide does not plan routes and does not say how long it takes to get from one place to another.

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, under the licence shown with each one. The map is © OpenStreetMap contributors.
