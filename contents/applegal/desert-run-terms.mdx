### What Desert Run is

### Price

Desert Run is free to download and play. It shows full-screen ads, which may be videos, and offers optional videos you choose to watch for a reward, such as a second chance or a gem. There are no ads during a race.

Gem packs and "No ads + 50 gems" are sold through Google Play, with the price shown by Google Play before you pay. Neither is a subscription.

- **Gems** are game currency. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money or transferred to another person, and are spent inside the game. Like the rest of your progress, they are kept on your phone, so uninstalling the app or resetting the phone removes them.
- **"No ads + 50 gems"** is a one-time purchase. It stops the full-screen ads for good, gives the rewards of the optional videos without the video, and adds 50 gems. The no-ads part is tied to the Google account that bought it: on another phone with the same account, or after reinstalling, the app turns it back on when it opens.
