Wrapped Love Stories reads a WhatsApp chat you export and tells the story of that conversation: how much you talk, when, which words and emoji are yours, and a video to share. The chat is read on your phone and is never sent to me or to any server. What does leave the phone is usage statistics, crash reports and, if you buy Premium, what Google Play needs to process the payment. This page says what each of those is.

I, Tiago Danin, am the developer and the controller of this data under the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018). If you are in the EU, the GDPR gives you the same rights and I answer them the same way. Write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com about any of it.

### What you give the app

#### The chat you export

You export a chat in WhatsApp ("Export chat", "Without media") and share it to the app, or pick the file in Android's file picker. The app needs no storage permission for either. If the export is a zip, the app takes only the chat's text out of it; photos, audio and videos inside are never opened.

The app copies the file into its own temporary storage, reads it in the background and deletes the copy as soon as the reading ends, whether it succeeded or not. The reading happens entirely on your phone. None of the chat is uploaded to me, to Google or to anyone else.

#### What is kept from it

After reading, the app keeps only what the story is told from, in its own private storage on your phone:

- counts and distributions: messages per person, per day, month, hour and weekday, streaks, the longest gap between messages, sessions, replies, media and link counts, and the dates when the conversation reached round numbers of messages (the 1,000th, the 10,000th and so on);
- the 30 most used words and the 20 most used emoji, and how many times certain phrases appear ("I love you", "miss you", "good morning", "sorry" and laughs), with the date of the first one and who sent it;
- the names of the people in the chat as they appear in the export, and the names you choose for the story;
- the first messages of the conversation, which one scene shows as notifications on a lock screen: up to four messages sent in the first three minutes, the first 160 characters of each, with who sent it and when;
- how you arranged the story: the order of the scenes, the ones you switched off, the ones shown as just a number, and the lock screen's background;
- if you choose "Your photo" as that background, a copy of the photo you pick. The app opens Android's picker for this, so it sees only the one photo you choose and needs no gallery permission.

The words, the first messages and the photo can include personal things. They never leave the phone unless you share a scene or the video yourself. You can delete one Wrapped from its menu on the home screen, or all of them in About, "Delete all Wrappeds". Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

#### Other people's messages

A chat has two people. The app processes the other person's messages too, on your phone only, to count them. Import only conversations you are entitled to look at, and think of the other person before you share a scene or the video, since it shows facts about both of you.

#### Videos and images you make

The video and the scene images are drawn on your phone. A video stays in the app's temporary storage until you share it or tap "Save to gallery", which puts it in a "Wrapped Love Stories" album in your gallery. When you share, it goes through Android's share sheet to the app you choose, and from there that app's own privacy policy applies.

### What the app collects on its own

#### Usage statistics

Google Analytics for Firebase records events such as opening the app, which screen is open, starting an import (from a share or the picker), whether it succeeded or why it failed, the size of the chat in broad ranges (for example "10k to 50k messages", "1 to 3 years"), how long the reading took, how many scenes a story has, finishing a story, sharing a scene (which kind of scene, never its content), making, cancelling, sharing or saving a video, seeing the Premium screen and whether a purchase went through. No event carries a name, a word, a message or anything else from your chat. The app does not create an account or send a user ID, so these events are tied to an app installation, not to a person. Analytics also sees your device model, operating system, app version and approximate location.

The app has no ads and does not read the device's advertising ID: it is removed from the app and its collection is turned off.

#### Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics receives a report when the app crashes or an error is caught: the error, the device model, the Android version and the state of the app at that moment. Reports never include the chat's content.

#### Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment: I never see your card or your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account has bought Premium, when you buy, when you tap "Restore purchase" and quietly when the app starts, so a purchase made on another phone is picked up. Google shares with me, through the Play Console, the order information it gives every developer, such as the order number, date, country and amount.

### What stays on your phone

Your saved Wrappeds as described above, and whether you have Premium. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Videos you saved to the gallery stay there until you delete them.

### Who else receives it

The app has no server of its own. What it sends goes to Google:

- **Google Analytics for Firebase** and **Firebase Crashlytics** receive the statistics and crash reports.
- **Google Play** processes the Premium payment and tells the app whether you own Premium.

Their terms: [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Google Play terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

I do not sell your data or hand it to a data broker. I would only disclose it if a Brazilian court or authority legally required it, and the chat itself is not something I could hand over: I never receive it.

### Why it is used

The chat is read only to build the story you asked for, at your request. Purchase data is used to deliver the Premium you paid for, which is the performance of that contract. The statistics and crash reports are used to find bugs and to see which parts of the app work, on the basis of my legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

### Where it is stored

Your chat and your Wrappeds stay on your phone. Google stores the statistics, crash reports and purchase data on its servers, which may be outside Brazil, including in the United States, and applies the safeguards described in its data processing terms to those transfers.

### How long it is kept

The exported file is deleted as soon as it has been read. Saved Wrappeds stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app. Analytics statistics and crash reports are kept for the retention period configured in Firebase, then Google deletes them. Google Play keeps its data under Google's own policies.

### Your rights

Because there is no account and the chat never reaches me, nothing on my side links data to you by name. You can still ask me what exists, ask for deletion, or object to processing at TiagoDanin@outlook.com, and I answer within 15 days, the period the LGPD sets. Deleting your Wrappeds in the app and uninstalling it remove what is on your phone. You may also complain to the ANPD, Brazil's data protection authority.

### Security

Your chat is processed in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and the temporary copy is deleted after reading. Traffic to Google's services is encrypted. No system is completely secure, but your chat is never on a server to begin with.

### Children

Wrapped Love Stories is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child's data reached the app, write to me.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. For a change that affects what is collected or who receives it, the app shows a notice before the change takes effect.
