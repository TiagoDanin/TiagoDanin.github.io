Virtual Pocket Air Conditioning collects no personal data. There is nothing to sign up for, nothing is saved on the phone, and the app does not even ask for permission to use the internet.

I, Tiago Danin, am the developer. Write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com about anything on this page.

### What the app does with your phone

It plays a bundled air conditioner sound in a loop and shows an animation. It asks Android for one permission, to keep the device awake while the sound plays, so the audio does not stop when the screen turns off.

It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no third-party SDK that collects data. Nothing you do in the app is recorded or sent anywhere.

### Links out

The About screen links to tiagodanin.com and to the page of the sound's author on orangefreesounds.com. Tapping one opens your browser, and from then on that site's own privacy policy applies.

### Your rights

Since I hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. The LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) still gives you the right to ask, and I answer at the address above.

### Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone, children included.

### Changes

If a future version starts collecting anything, this page will say so before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
