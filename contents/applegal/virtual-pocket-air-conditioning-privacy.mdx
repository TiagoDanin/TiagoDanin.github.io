Virtual Pocket Air Conditioning has no account, no ads and nothing to sign up for. It plays a sound. The only data that leaves the phone is a set of anonymous usage statistics.

I, Tiago Danin, am the developer and the controller of this data under the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018). If you are in the EU, the GDPR gives you the same rights and I answer them the same way. Write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com about any of it.

### What the app does with your phone

It plays a bundled air conditioner sound in a loop and shows an animation. It asks Android to keep the device awake while the sound plays, so the audio does not stop when the screen turns off. It saves nothing on the phone.

### What the app sends

Google Analytics for Firebase records how the app is used: when you turn the air conditioner on or off and for how long it ran, when it reaches full volume, when you leave or come back to the app with it on, when the sound fails to play, when you open the About screen and which link you tap there. Like any app using the SDK, it also records automatic events such as the first open and each session, together with an ID for this installation, the device model, Android version, app version and approximate location. The SDK may read the install referrer (how you got to the app on Google Play). The app turns off the advertising ID, so the SDK neither reads nor sends it. The app does not send a user ID, because it has none, and does not use this data for advertising.

For this, the app now uses the internet connection.

### Who else receives it

Google, which runs Firebase and processes this data on my behalf, on servers that may be outside Brazil, including in the United States. Their terms: [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

I do not sell this data, share it with advertisers, or hand it to a data broker. The statistics are used to find bugs and to see which parts of the app people use, on the basis of my legitimate interest in keeping it working.

### Links out

The About screen links to tiagodanin.com, to this page and to the page of the sound's author on orangefreesounds.com. Tapping one opens your browser, and from then on that site's own privacy policy applies.

### How long it is kept

Statistics are kept for the retention period configured in Firebase, after which Google deletes them.

### Your rights

Since the statistics carry no name or account, I usually cannot tell which ones are yours. You can still ask me what exists, ask me to delete it, or object to it, at TiagoDanin@outlook.com. You may also complain to the ANPD, Brazil's data protection authority.

### Children

The app is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child's data reached me, write to me and I delete what I can find.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change in what is collected is described here before the version that makes it is published.
