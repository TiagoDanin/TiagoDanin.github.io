### What SwipeClean is

A tool to free space on your phone and tidy its gallery: you review photos and videos one at a time, keep them or mark them for deletion, move them into albums, and delete what you marked after reviewing it. It works on your phone's own gallery and needs your permission to read it.

### Your decisions and your files

Every deletion and every move is your decision. A swipe only marks a photo; nothing is deleted until you confirm on the "Para excluir" screen and Android confirms again. On Android 11 and later deleted items go to Android's trash, where the gallery app can restore them for the period Android keeps them; on older versions, and once Android empties its trash, a deletion is permanent. The groups the app suggests (large files, near duplicates, screenshots, blurry and dark photos) are estimates computed on the phone, and they can include a photo that matters to you. Look before you confirm. Keep a backup of what you cannot lose.
