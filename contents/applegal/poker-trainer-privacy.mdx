Poker Trainer has no account and no server of its own. The lessons ship inside the app, and your progress stays on your phone. What leaves it is a set of anonymous usage statistics, your total XP for the ranking only if you sign in to Google Play Games, and the questions the app asks Google Play about the unlimited lives purchase.

I, Tiago Danin, am the developer and the controller of this data under the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018). If you are in the EU, the GDPR gives you the same rights and I answer them the same way. Write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com about any of it.

### What stays on your phone

The app saves your progress: which lessons you finished, your best accuracy and number of attempts on each, your XP, chips, lives and streak, the days you played, the lesson you left halfway, whether you bought unlimited lives, and counters of answers and of the kinds of mistakes you make, which feed the tips at the end of a lesson. That is what lets you pick up where you stopped.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, erases all of it.

### What the app sends

Google Analytics for Firebase records how the app is used: lessons started, finished or left halfway (with the lesson, its phase and a rough accuracy), each answer (the lesson, the kind of exercise and whether it was right), running out of lives, opening an exercise's extra notes, the tips shown at the end of a lesson, finishing a phase, streak milestones, chips earned and spent, free practice, opening the ranking or sending a score to it, and seeing the unlimited lives offer, the outcome of a purchase (bought, cancelled, pending or failed) and of a "Restore purchase". Like any app using the SDK, it also records automatic events such as the first open, each session and the screens you visit, together with an ID for this installation, the device model, operating system, app version and approximate location. On Android the SDK may read the install referrer (how you got to the app on Google Play). The app turns off the advertising ID, so the SDK neither reads nor sends it. The app does not send a user ID, because it has none, and does not use this data for advertising.

Lessons work offline. The statistics wait and go out when there is a connection.

### Google Play Games ranking

On Android the app can show a ranking of players. It uses Google Play Games, which signs you in with the Play Games profile already on the phone. When you are signed in, your total XP goes to a Play Games leaderboard, where other players see it next to your Play Games name and avatar. Google runs that profile and that leaderboard under its own terms, and you can hide or delete your Play Games activity in the Play Games app. If you never sign in, nothing goes there and the app works the same.

### Unlimited lives purchase

Unlimited lives is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment: I never see your card or your payment details. The app asks Google Play for the price, and whether your Google account has bought unlimited lives when you buy, when you tap "Restore purchase" and quietly when the app starts, so a purchase made on another phone is picked up. Google shares with me, through the Play Console, the order information it gives every developer, such as the order number, date, country and amount. If you never buy, nothing about a purchase is recorded.

### Who else receives it

Google, which runs Firebase and processes this data on my behalf, on servers that may be outside Brazil, including in the United States. Google Play also processes the unlimited lives payment and tells the app whether you own it. Their terms: [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), [Google Play terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

I do not sell this data, share it with advertisers, or hand it to a data broker. The statistics are used to find bugs and to see which lessons are too hard or too easy, on the basis of my legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. Purchase data is used to deliver the unlimited lives you paid for, which is the performance of that contract, and order records are kept as tax law requires. They are kept for the retention period configured in Firebase, after which Google deletes them.

### Your rights

Since the statistics carry no name or account, I usually cannot tell which ones are yours. You can still ask me what exists, ask me to delete it, or object to it, at TiagoDanin@outlook.com. You may also complain to the ANPD, Brazil's data protection authority.

### Children

The app teaches a card game and is meant for adults. I do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child's data reached me, write to me and I delete what I can find.

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change in what is collected is described here before the version that makes it is published.
