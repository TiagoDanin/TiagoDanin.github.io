### What Direção Fácil is, and is not

A study app for the theory exam of the Brazilian driver's licence (CNH). It has learning trails on traffic legislation, road signs, defensive driving, first aid, basic mechanics and infractions, and multiple-choice questions in the style of the DETRAN exams, based on the Código de Trânsito Brasileiro.

Direção Fácil is not affiliated with DETRAN, SENATRAN or any government body, and it is not an official source. Studying with it does not guarantee that you pass. The questions and explanations may contain errors or fall behind a change in the law: when they disagree with the official legislation, the legislation prevails. If you find a mistake, tell me and I fix it.

### Price, lives and Premium

The app is free to download and study with. Answering uses lives: you have up to five, and one comes back every five minutes.

When you run out, you can wait, watch a rewarded ad to get one life back, or buy Premium, which gives unlimited lives. Watching an ad is always your choice.

Premium is a one-time purchase paid through Google Play. It is not a subscription and does not renew. The app shows the price Google Play gives it before you confirm. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy and the rights the Código de Defesa do Consumidor gives you.

The purchase belongs to your Google Play account. On a new phone the app finds it by itself; if it does not, tap Restore purchase (Restaurar compra) on the Premium offer. If it still does not show up, write to me.
