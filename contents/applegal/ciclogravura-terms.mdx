### What Ciclogravura is

A game inspired by the Brazilian craft of drawing landscapes in a glass bottle with coloured sand. You pour, shape and remove sand, tilt the phone, place small items, keep finished bottles in a collection and share their pictures. A campaign of levels asks you to recreate a reference landscape and grades the result in stars.
