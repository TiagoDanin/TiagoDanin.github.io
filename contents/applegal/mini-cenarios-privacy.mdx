Mini Cenários is a game about decorating small isometric rooms, like the sticker scene books. Your rooms, your coins and your progress stay on your phone. What leaves it is usage statistics, crash reports, what Google's ad network needs when you choose to watch an ad, and, if you buy Premium, what Google Play needs to process the payment. This page says what each of those is.

I, Tiago Danin, am the developer and the controller of this data under the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018). If you are in the EU, the GDPR gives you the same rights and I answer them the same way. Write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com about any of it.

### What you make in the app

#### Your rooms

The pieces you place in each room, where they stand and which way they face, the walls and floor you choose, the tasks you completed, the rooms you create in free mode and the names you give them are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. None of it is uploaded to me or to any server.

#### Sharing

When you share a room, a picture of it with its name and the name of the game goes through Android's share sheet to the app you choose. From there, that app's own privacy policy applies.

### What the app collects on its own

#### Usage statistics

Google Analytics for Firebase records events such as opening a room, placing or removing a piece, undoing, completing a task, opening a piece from the moving box, finishing a room, sharing it, creating or deleting a free room, changing walls or floor, turning music or effects on or off, buying a decoration with coins, claiming an achievement, starting and completing a rewarded ad, and buying, cancelling or restoring Premium. These events carry only the ids of the game's own rooms and pieces and simple counts. The names you give rooms are never sent, and rooms you create are sent only as "free". The app does not create an account or send a user ID, so these events are tied to an app installation, not to a name. Analytics also sees your device model, operating system, app version and approximate location, and it may read the device's advertising ID.

#### Crash reports

Firebase Crashlytics receives a report when the app crashes or an error is caught: the error, the device model, the Android version and the state of the app at that moment.

#### Ads

Mini Cenários shows an ad only when you ask for one: a room can open early, and some decorations can be unlocked, after you watch a short rewarded video. There are no banners and no ads that appear on their own. Google AdMob serves the video. To pick and measure ads, AdMob uses the advertising ID, your IP address, the device model and how you interact with the ad, and it may show ads based on your interests. With Premium no ad is ever shown.

Where the law asks for consent, such as in the EU and the UK, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad loads, and you can change your answer later in About, "Ad privacy". You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings, under Privacy, Ads (on some phones, Google, Ads). Deleting it stops personalised ads on the whole device.

#### Premium purchase

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment: I never see your card or your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account has bought Premium, when you buy, when you tap "Restore purchase" and quietly when the app starts, so a purchase made on another phone is picked up. Google shares with me, through the Play Console, the order information it gives every developer, such as the order number, date, country and amount.

Coins are earned by playing and are never sold.

### What stays on your phone

Your rooms and everything in them, your free rooms and their names, your coins, the decorations you bought, your achievements, how many times you shared, which rooms and decorations you opened with an ad, whether you have Premium and whether music and effects are on. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Pictures you shared stay wherever you sent them.

### Who else receives it

The app has no server of its own. What it sends goes to Google:

- **Google AdMob** receives what it needs to show the rewarded ads you ask for and to record your consent choice, as described above.
- **Google Play** processes the Premium payment and tells the app whether you own Premium.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** and **Firebase Crashlytics** receive the statistics and crash reports.

Their terms: [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), [Google Play terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

I do not sell your data or hand it to a data broker. I would only disclose it if a Brazilian court or authority legally required it.

### Why it is used

The rewarded ads you choose to watch and Premium pay for the app. The ads rely on your consent where the law requires it, and otherwise on my legitimate interest and on the choices you make in Android's ad settings. Purchase data is used to deliver the Premium you paid for, which is the performance of that contract. The statistics and crash reports are used to find bugs and to see which rooms, pieces and tasks people use, on the basis of my legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

### Where it is stored

Google stores this data on its servers, which may be outside Brazil, including in the United States. Google applies the safeguards described in its data processing terms to those transfers.

### How long it is kept

Analytics statistics and crash reports are kept for the retention period configured in Firebase, then Google deletes them. AdMob and Google Play keep their data under Google's own policies.

### Your rights

Because there is no account, nothing on my side links data to you by name. You can still ask me what exists, ask for deletion, or object to processing at TiagoDanin@outlook.com, and I answer within 15 days, the period the LGPD sets. Resetting the advertising ID and uninstalling the app cut the link between new data and your device. You may also complain to the ANPD, Brazil's data protection authority.

### Security

Traffic to Google's services is encrypted. No system is completely secure, but your rooms are never on a server to begin with.

### Children

Mini Cenários is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child's data reached the app, write to me.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. For a change that affects what is collected or who receives it, the app shows a notice before the change takes effect.
