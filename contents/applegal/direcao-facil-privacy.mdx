Direção Fácil is a study app for the Brazilian driver's licence (CNH) theory exam. To keep your progress between sessions and phones it needs an account, and to pay for itself it shows optional ads and sells Premium. This page says what the app stores, who else touches it and how to have it deleted.

I, Tiago Danin, am the developer and the controller of this data under the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018). If you are in the EU, the GDPR gives you the same rights and I answer them the same way. Write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com about any of it.

### What you give the app

#### The first questions

Before you sign in, the app asks five questions: your goal, how much you already know, how long you can study a day, your main difficulty and how you like to learn. The answers are saved under a random ID created for that session. They are not linked to your account, your name or your email.

#### Your account

Studying requires signing in, with Google or with an email and password. The account holds your email, the name you type when registering and, with Google, your profile photo.

#### Your progress

Your profile stores your XP and level, your streak and the days you studied, achievements, completed modules, lessons and trails, daily quests, weekly XP, perfect quizzes, the questions you got wrong and whether the study reminder is on.

### What the app collects on its own

#### Usage statistics

Google Analytics for Firebase records the screens you open and events such as starting and finishing a quiz, answering a question, leaving a quiz, finishing a lesson, keeping or losing a streak, running out of lives, signing in or out, finishing or skipping onboarding, watching an ad, buying or restoring Premium and deleting the account. The app does not send your account ID to Analytics. Analytics also sees your device model, operating system, app version and approximate location, and it may read the device's advertising ID.

#### Crash reports and performance

Firebase Crashlytics receives a report when the app crashes: the error, the device model, the Android version and the state of the app at that moment. Firebase Performance Monitoring measures how long screens and network requests take.

#### Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging registers a token for your device. Direção Fácil sends no push messages today and does not ask for notification permission.

#### Ads

The only ads are rewarded ones: when you run out of lives you can choose to watch an ad to get one back. Nothing plays unless you tap it. Before the first ad, where the law of the place you are in requires it, the app shows Google's consent form, and you can change your answer later in Profile, Ad privacy (Privacidade dos anúncios). Google AdMob serves them, and to pick and measure ads it uses the advertising ID, your IP address, the device model and how you interact with the ad. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings, under Privacy, Ads (on some phones, Google, Ads).

#### Premium

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. The purchase belongs to your Google Play account, not to your app account: Direção Fácil only asks Google Play whether it exists. No other service is involved, and I never see your card details.

### What stays on your phone

Your cached name, email and profile photo address, your sound and vibration preferences, whether you bought Premium, your lives and their timers, the last day quests were generated, and whether onboarding is done. Uninstalling the app removes them.

### Who else receives it

The app has no server of its own besides the images below:

- **Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore** (Google) hold your account and progress. Security rules let only your signed-in account read or write your profile.
- **Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config and Cloud Messaging** receive the statistics, reports and tokens described above.
- **Google Sign-In**, if you choose it, confirms your identity with your Google account.
- **Google AdMob** receives what it needs to show the rewarded ads.
- **Google Play** processes the Premium purchase.
- The traffic sign images in the questions are loaded from direcaofacil.com, which receives your IP address like any web server.

Their terms: [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

I do not sell your data or hand it to a data broker. I would only disclose it if a Brazilian court or authority legally required it.

### Why it is used

To run the service you asked for: keep your account and progress, build the trails and quests, and give you Premium when you pay for it. The ads and Premium pay for the app. The statistics and crash reports are used to find bugs and see which parts of the course people use, on the basis of my legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

### Where it is stored

Google stores data on servers that may be outside Brazil, including in the United States, and applies the safeguards described in its data processing terms to those transfers.

### How long it is kept

Your account and progress exist for as long as the account does. The onboarding answers are not linked to anyone and are kept to improve the course. Analytics statistics and crash reports are kept for the retention period configured in Firebase. AdMob and Google Play keep their own records under their own policies, and Google Play keeps purchase records for as long as tax and consumer law require.

### Deleting your account and your other rights

In the app, open Profile (Perfil) and tap Delete account (Excluir conta). The app asks you to confirm, with your password or by picking your Google account again, and right away deletes your profile and progress from Firestore, your account from Firebase Authentication and what was stored on the phone. If you cannot get into the app, write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com from the account's email and ask for deletion: within 15 days I delete all of that and reply to confirm. Signing out does not delete anything. Premium belongs to your Google Play account and is not deleted with it.

You can also ask me what data exists about you, to correct it, to receive a copy, or to withdraw consent. I answer within 15 days, the period the LGPD sets. You may also complain to the ANPD, Brazil's data protection authority.

### Security

Traffic to Firebase and Google Play is encrypted, and access to your profile is limited to your account by security rules. No system is completely secure, and I cannot promise that nobody will ever break in, but I will tell you and the ANPD if an incident puts your data at risk.

### Children

Direção Fácil is meant for people preparing for the driver's licence, which in Brazil starts at 18. It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, write to me and I delete it.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. For a change that affects what is collected or who receives it, the app shows a notice before the change takes effect.
