### What Wrapped Love Stories is

An app that reads a WhatsApp chat between two people that you export, and tells the story of that conversation in full-screen scenes: how long you have talked, how much, when, which words and emoji are yours, and other patterns. It then makes a vertical video of the story that you can save and share. The chat is read on your phone.

### Price

Wrapped Love Stories is free to download and use. Without Premium, a story shows its first scenes and then says how many more there are, and the video is shorter, in 720p, with a small "Wrapped Love Stories" mark.

### The numbers

Every number and sentence in a story is computed automatically from the exported text, never written by hand. WhatsApp exports differ between phones, languages and versions, so a count can be off in some cases, for example when messages were deleted, when media were left out of the export, or when the export itself is incomplete. The story is for fun and for memories, not a record to rely on.
