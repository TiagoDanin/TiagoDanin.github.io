### What Mini Cenários is

A cozy game inspired by the sticker scene books. You unpack pieces from a moving box and decorate small isometric rooms: place, drag, turn and remove furniture, plants, pets and decor, change walls and floors, and complete each room's tasks. In free mode you create your own rooms. Finished rooms go to a gallery, where you can share their pictures.

### Price

Mini Cenários is free to download and play. Rooms open one after another as you finish them, and you earn coins by playing, which buy decorations in the shop. A room can open early, and some decorations can be unlocked, after you choose to watch a short rewarded ad. Coins are never sold.
