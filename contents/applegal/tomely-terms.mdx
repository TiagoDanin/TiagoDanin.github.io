### What Tomely is, and is not

What you enter is what it reminds you of, so check each schedule against your prescription. Decisions about your treatment belong to you and your doctor or pharmacist. ### Reminders depend on your phone

Reminders are scheduled on the device. Battery savers, Do Not Disturb, a denied notification or alarm permission, a phone that is off or a manufacturer that kills background apps can delay or block them. The app asks for the permissions it needs, but I cannot guarantee that every reminder will ring on time. Do not rely on Tomely as the only safeguard for a dose that cannot be missed.
