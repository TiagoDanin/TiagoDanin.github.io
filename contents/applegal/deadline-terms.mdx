### What Deadline is

A turn-based game of chance and nerve. A shotgun is loaded with live and blank shells in a hidden order, and on your turn you shoot the masked Dealer or yourself, use items, and try to outlast three rounds (five on the Insane level). It is fiction: no real weapon, no real money and no prize of any kind is involved.

### Price

Deadline is free to download and play. It shows ads: a full-screen ad at most once every three defeats, and a rewarded video you may choose to watch on the defeat screen for one extra life in that run. Watching it is never required.

Premium is a one-time purchase sold through Google Play, with the price shown by Google Play before you pay. It is not a subscription. It removes the full-screen ads and gives the extra life without a video. It never changes the odds, the shells or the Dealer. It is tied to the Google account that bought it: on another phone with the same account, "Restore purchase" in the app turns it back on.
