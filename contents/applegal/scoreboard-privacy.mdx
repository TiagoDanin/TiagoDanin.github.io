Scoreboard collects no personal data. It has no account, no server and no internet permission. The match you are scoring and your settings stay on your phone.

I, Tiago Danin, am the developer. Write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com about anything on this page.

### What stays on your phone

The app saves the current match (its rules, the list of points scored, when it started and which side each team is on), the rules of your last match, the winning streak and your preferences for voice, sound, vibration and the introduction screens. That is what lets it resume a match after you close it. Teams are always Blue and Red: the app asks for no names and no photos.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, erases all of it.

### What the app uses on the device

- **Spoken score**, off by default. When you turn it on, the score text goes to the phone's own speech engine, the one set in your Android or iOS settings.
- **Screen always on** while the match screen is open, released when you leave it.
- **Sharing**, only when you tap it. The app hands the system share sheet a line of text such as "Blue 21 x 18 Red", and you choose where it goes.

There are no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads and no third-party SDK that collects data.

### Your rights

Since I hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. The LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018) still gives you the right to ask, and I answer at the address above.

### Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone, children included.

### Changes

If a future version starts collecting anything, this page will say so before that version is published, and the date at the top will change.
