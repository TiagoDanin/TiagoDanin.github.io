Read anything here. Quote it with credit. Reuse the code under its licence. Do not republish my writing as your own. The rest is detail.

### What this site is

A personal site: articles, talks, a list of projects and a CV. It is not a product, it comes with no warranty, and nothing on it is an offer of contract. Work gets hired through a signed agreement, and the [company page](/business) explains how that runs.

### The code

The source of this site is public on GitHub under the MIT licence. That covers the code. It does not cover the articles, the photographs, the talk slides, or my name and picture.

### The writing

Articles and talk material stay my copyright. Quote a few paragraphs with a link back and we have no problem. Republishing a whole piece, translating it, or feeding it into something you sell needs my written permission first.

### What you agree not to do

Do not attack the site, scrape it hard enough to cost bandwidth for no reason, or put someone else's email into the feedback form. Security research is welcome, and it is a good part of what I do elsewhere: send a finding to my email before you send it anywhere else.

### Links out

Pages link to GitHub, npm, YouTube, press coverage and other people's sites. I control none of them, and a link is not an endorsement.

### Accuracy

Project counts, download numbers and stars come from public APIs at the moment the site is built, so they lag by however long it has been since the last deploy. Dates and job titles are right as far as I know. If you find something wrong, tell me and I fix it.
