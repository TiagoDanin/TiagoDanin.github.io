This site is a pile of static files. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into, no database of visitors. Almost nothing about you is collected, and the little that is passes through services you already know.

I am the controller of the personal data described here, under the LGPD (Lei 13.709/2018). If you are in the EU, the GDPR gives you the same rights and I answer them the same way. Write to TiagoDanin@outlook.com about any of it.

### What gets collected

#### Audience measurement

Google Analytics counts page views so I know which articles are worth writing more of. It sees which page you opened, where you arrived from, your rough location and your browser, and it leaves a cookie behind. Any content blocker stops it, and Google publishes an opt-out extension if you would rather not install one.

#### Hosting

The site runs on GitHub Pages. GitHub's servers receive your IP address and browser when they send you a page, the way any web server does. Those logs belong to GitHub and I never see them.

#### Comments

Comments under articles and talks run on GitHub Discussions. Nothing leaves your browser until you sign in with GitHub. If you do comment, your username, avatar and text are public in the site's repository, and you can edit or delete them later without asking me.

#### Video

Talk pages that have a recording embed the YouTube player, which loads from Google and can leave cookies of its own. Pages without a video load nothing from YouTube.

#### The feedback form

After a talk I hand out a link to a short form. It asks for your email, four ratings and anything you want to tell me. That is the one thing on the site I actually receive and read, and nothing goes anywhere until you press submit.

Google, GitHub and Cloudflare handle what reaches them under their own terms: [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement), [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### What the site keeps on your device

If you dismiss the banner offering the Portuguese version, your browser remembers that so the site stops asking. Nothing else is stored, and clearing site data clears it.

### Your rights

You can ask me what data exists about you, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent. Write to the address above and I answer.

Data held by Google or GitHub is a separate matter. Use their own tools, because I cannot reach their records for you.

### Children

There is nothing here to sign up for, the site is not aimed at children, and I do not knowingly collect anything from someone under 13.

### What I do not do

I do not sell your data, rent it, or hand it to a data broker.
