As LLMs began to evolve, I have been exploring how to use these resources to build products end-to-end, from idea validation to prototyping and full development, ready for production in much shorter timeframes.

In this talk, I will present my workflow process with these tools, showing how I structure this flow in practice and how I develop some of my apps. I will also share the case of a Duolingo-style app, but focused on people pursuing their first driver's license, showing the decisions, tools, and lessons learned along the way using this workflow.
